**Journal:** Scientific Reports. Item numbers and titles follow the official STROBE Statement checklist (von Elm 2007) 【16】. Locations refer to `眼科2.md`; supporting diagnostics in `Supplementary_audit.md`.

**Notes.** (i) Prospective registration: this analysis was pre-specified in a frozen analysis plan (`admission_record.md`) but was not registered in a public trial registry; reported under Item 12 (statistical methods), not as a separate STROBE item. (ii) All effect estimates and CIs are design-based (Taylor linearisation) with degrees of freedom = design df less model rank (NHANES 22, NHIS 598); the delete-a-PSU jackknife was recomputed on the identical model/sample only as a variance concordance check (S-C). (iii) No numbers were hand-entered; every value in Tables 1–4 and Figures 1–3 is generated by the scripts mapped in `Supplementary_audit.md` S-A.
